# 研究プロファイル

## 1. 関心のある研究領域

高柳匡研究室に所属している。以下の領域の新着を幅広く追いたい。

### TTbar変形とその p乗 / (1/p)乗一般化
TTbar deformation, JTbar / TJbar deformation, root-TTbar, irrelevant deformation,
cutoff AdS, finite cutoff holography, Dirichlet wall, holographic screen,
Zamolodchikov factorization, deformed spectrum / Burgers equation, TTbar and
de Sitter holography, TsT transformation, single-trace TTbar, entanglement entropy
under TTbar

### Gödel時空のホログラフィー
Gödel spacetime, closed timelike curve (CTC), holographic screen, preferred screen,
causal structure and holography, rotating spacetime, non-AdS holography,
stretched horizon, causality violation

### エンタングルメント・エントロピーとホログラフィー(中核)
Ryu-Takayanagi formula, HRT / covariant HEE, quantum extremal surface, generalized
entropy, entanglement wedge, entanglement wedge reconstruction, subregion duality,
entanglement wedge cross section, entanglement of purification, reflected entropy,
multi-entropy, Renyi entropy, modular Hamiltonian, relative entropy, strong
subadditivity, g-theorem, quantum information metric, entanglement density

### pseudo entropy / timelike EE / 非エルミート(近年の中心テーマ・特に重視)
pseudo entropy, holographic pseudo entropy, topological pseudo entropy, thermal
pseudo-entropy, timelike entanglement entropy, non-hermitian density matrix,
imagitivity, SVD entanglement entropy, PT symmetry / PT symmetry breaking,
transition matrix, post-selection, entanglement phase transition

### AdS/BCFT・境界と欠陥
AdS/BCFT, boundary conformal field theory, end-of-the-world brane (EOW brane),
Cardy state, boundary state, brane-localized field, boundary condition changing
operator, brane dynamics, wedge holography, codimension-two holography, double
holography, island formula, brane cosmology, defect CFT

### de Sitter ホログラフィー
dS/CFT, half de Sitter holography, static patch holography, dS entropy, island in
de Sitter, Chern-Simons gravity dual of dS3, two-time geometry / Lorentzian torus,
Hartle-Hawking wave function, complex geodesic

### 平坦時空・セレスティアル
flat space holography, Carrollian CFT (CCFT), celestial holography, BMS symmetry,
swing surface, null boundary, flat limit of AdS/CFT

### 時空の創発・テンソルネットワーク
emergent spacetime from quantum information, cMERA, tensor network,
surface/state correspondence, holographic renormalization group, p-adic AdS/CFT,
quantum circuit interpretation of holography, quantum error correction in holography

### ブラックホール情報問題・ダイナミクス
Page curve, island, Hayden-Preskill protocol, information recovery, scrambling,
OTOC, local quench, joining/splitting quench, local operator quench, quantum
teleportation in holography, traversable wormhole, AdS wormhole, double trace
deformation, Janus deformation, ETH, eternal BTZ

### 周辺分野(重み: 中)
